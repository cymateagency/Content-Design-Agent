# Funnel playbook for AWA

Audience: agency owners and leaders (marketing, creative, digital, PR agencies) who care about keeping their people and being known as a great place to work. Every post sits at one stage.

## TOF (top of funnel): awareness
- Goal: reach, saves, shares. Make agency leaders feel "that's so true."
- Content: culture truths, hot takes, relatable agency moments, quiet quitting signs, humour.
- Never sell. Never mention pricing, the award, or applying.
- CTA: soft. "Save this", "Send this to your co-founder", "Follow for more".
- Templates that have worked: `poster-lockscreen`, `poster-report-card`, `scrapbook-carousel`, `poster-glass-photo`, `poster-scroll-phone`.

## MOF (middle of funnel): consideration
- Goal: show AWA understands culture and how recognition works. Build trust.
- Content: how-tos, frameworks, culture pillars, what the culture rating tiers mean, Behind the Agency features, award-winner spotlights, leader quotes (real ones only).
- Light AWA mentions are fine.
- CTA: "Click the link in our bio to see how agencies get rated", "Read the full story", comment prompts.
- Templates that have worked: the ghost-word carousel (`carousel-cover` / `carousel-content` / `carousel-cta`), `poster-glass-photo`, `poster-soft-glow`.

## BOF (bottom of funnel): conversion
- Goal: nominations, applications, sign-ups.
- Content: nominations open, deadlines, what winners get, the badge, testimonials from certified agencies (real ones only).
- CTA: direct. "Nominate your agency", "Get recognized today", "Apply by <date>", link in bio.
- Templates that have worked: `poster-ticket`, `poster-glass-photo` with a hard CTA, `poster-soft-glow` with a "Nominate your agency" pill.
- Award name, year, nomination dates, deadlines and prices always come from the request, never from memory or past posts. If the request doesn't have them, use `[YEAR]` / `[DATE]` placeholders and flag them.

## Topic bank (starting points)
TOF: why good people leave, quiet signs of disengagement, perks vs. real culture, "we're like a family", Monday energy, burnout in agency life.
MOF: recognition rhythms, 3 pillars of culture, what culture rating tiers mean, how to run a feedback loop, spotlight on a certified agency.
BOF: nominations open, deadline reminders, what the badge does for hiring, winner reveal.

## Planning a batch or a week
Plan before making anything, show the plan, and wait for an OK.
- Mix: roughly 50% TOF, 30% MOF, 20% BOF unless asked otherwise (5 posts = 2–3 TOF, 1–2 MOF, 1 BOF).
- No topic repeated from the last 4 weeks of the content log (a different angle on the same topic still counts as a repeat).
- Layouts are fresh by default; at least half the plan is new layouts; never the same layout twice in a row.
- Hooks follow the voice: direct, warm, a little cheeky, under 12 words.
- Flag anything the user needs to supply (data, real quotes, dates, photos).

Return a table in posting order: `| # | Day | Stage | Layout (new: one-line sketch, or template name) | Topic | Hook |`, then the stage mix, the layout mix, topics skipped and what's needed.
