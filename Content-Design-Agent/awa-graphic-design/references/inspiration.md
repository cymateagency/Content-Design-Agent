# Inspiration board

Approved references live in the awa-posts project under `references/<stage>/` (tof, mof, bof). They are other brands' posts, so they are **not** copied into this skill: open them there, or ask the user to share the one they mean. Before designing, look at the references for the post's stage, pick the closest, and say in one line what you're borrowing (layout, hierarchy, a device). Borrow structure, never the other brand's look, colours, copy, logo or subject. If the folder for a stage is empty, say so and design from the brand kit and `taste.md`.

## What makes them work (apply to every AWA post)
- **One hero, lots of air.** The person, device or object owns 40–60% of the canvas.
- **Weight contrast in type.** Light setup, bold payoff, one accent word.
- **Real-looking materials.** Glass with blur and a bright top edge, paper with a crisp shadow, devices with real UI conventions.
- **Light, not decoration.** The red glow sits behind the hero like a light source.

## Guardrails
- If someone could put the two side by side and call it a copy, change it.
- AWA colours only: references in green, teal or any other colour get rebuilt in red, black and white.
