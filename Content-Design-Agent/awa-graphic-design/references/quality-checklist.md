# Quality checklist

View every rendered PNG (every slide for carousels) and fix anything that fails before anyone sees it. The review (SKILL.md step 6) uses the same list and returns PASS or up to 6 fixes, most important first.

## Layout
- [ ] render.py printed no WARN lines (or each one was checked and is intentional).
- [ ] Nothing cut off at slide edges, overflowing its box, or overlapping other text or the logo (seamless objects meant to cross edges are fine; text never crosses).
- [ ] Headline has no widow or orphan punctuation.
- [ ] One clear focal point and a real-world element (photo, device, paper, UI) or strong depth.
- [ ] Max 3 text levels on a static.
- [ ] Safe zones respected for stories, Reels and banners (`formats.md`).

## Brand
- [ ] Only AWA Red `#CF0101`, black and white (plus their transparencies); Bright red `#FF3B3B` only for red text on dark; Paper `#F6F1EA` only in scrapbook and letter posts. No other hue anywhere (QA's off-palette check is quiet).
- [ ] Poppins, plus Anton for one statement headline at most and Caveat for short notes only (never in a headline); no browser default font showing.
- [ ] The real AWA logo, right version for the background (white words on dark, black words on white, all white on red), small, undistorted, never retyped.
- [ ] The red glow is a light source, not a flat block; at most one texture.
- [ ] No emoji in the design.
- [ ] Layout differs from the previous post; covers alternate dark/white across the feed.

## Honesty
- [ ] No statistics, percentages or studies unless the brief supplied them. `XX` placeholders flagged.
- [ ] No invented testimonials or quotes; real names, titles and quotes only when supplied.
- [ ] Invented first names only in fictional formats, never with job titles or company names.
- [ ] BOF details (award, year, deadline) come from the brief or are flagged placeholders.

## Hook and stage
- [ ] Would an agency owner stop scrolling? Specific, under 12 words, lands in the first second, names who and what.
- [ ] Clearly about agency life and recognition.
- [ ] CTA matches the stage: TOF soft (save, share, follow) and never mentions the award, pricing or applying; MOF learn more or comment; BOF direct (nominate, apply, deadline). The CTA spells out the action.

## Carousels
- [ ] Cover works on its own (hook, no body paragraph).
- [ ] One idea per slide; page counts correct.
- [ ] CTA slide lean: statement + one CTA + URL.
- [ ] PDF opens with slides in order.

## Text
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked on the images and the captions; curly quotes; no em dashes; no double spaces.

## Caption
- [ ] Matches the caption format in `voice-and-captions.md` (STAGE/FORMAT line, hook, body, CTA, 3–5 hashtags including #AgencyWorkplaces, the LinkedIn version with the link in the comments, Photos needed).
- [ ] Not a duplicate of something in the content log.

## Taste
- [ ] Follows every rule in `taste.md`. When flagging one, name the rule.
