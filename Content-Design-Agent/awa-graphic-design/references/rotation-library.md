## Devices to try next
- Resignation letter with the real reason circled (TOF)
- Checkbox poll, "vote in the comments" (MOF)
- Mirror split: what leaders say vs. what teams hear (MOF)
- A red connecting line snaking across carousel slides (MOF)
- Spotlight: leader photo, red name bar, diamond pattern (MOF, real people only)
- Zoom reveal: a tiny detail on slide 1, magnified on slide 2
- Page peel: a red page curling back to reveal the team underneath

## Patterns
- **Reveal mechanics win**: zoom, peel, crossing elements. Plan at least one per batch.
- **Real objects + people** read as human-made.
- **Comment-keyword CTAs** ("Comment PROOF and we'll send the link", CP-002) drive engagement: only promise things the team can actually send.
