# Style library

Pick the style that makes the idea land fastest, and vary it: don't use the same layout or style twice in a row (check `content-log.md`). Fresh layouts are the default; the templates are examples, not molds.

| Style | Template | Best for | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Soft glow** (default look) | `poster-soft-glow.html` | Any stage: a big rounded headline over red light with a real person or crowd rising out of it | Warm, premium |
| **Glass photo** | `poster-glass-photo.html` | A real team photo carries the feeling; frosted panel holds the line | Human, credible |
| **Lock screen / UI story** | `poster-lockscreen.html` | Tiny stories told in notifications, chats, calendars | Relatable, a little dark-funny |
| **Report card / scorecard** | `poster-report-card.html` | Grading perks vs. what matters | Cheeky |
| **Ticket / pass** | `poster-ticket.html` | BOF: nominations, deadlines, the award | Celebratory |
| **Device mid-scroll** | `poster-scroll-phone.html` | First impressions, careers pages, employer brand | Smart, urgent |
| **Ghost word** | `carousel-content.html` | MOF lessons: one outlined word per slide | Editorial |
| **Scrapbook** | `scrapbook-carousel/gen_scrapbook.py` | TOF stories that pull the swipe across slides | Personal, tactile |

## More directions to build from the same parts
- **Letter / note**: a resignation letter with the real reason circled, a thank-you note that never got sent.
- **Split screen**: what leaders say vs. what teams hear; perks vs. culture; then vs. now.
- **Pennant**: one huge line with a white pennant ribbon on the word that matters.
- **Spotlight**: a real leader photo with the red name bar and diamond pattern (real names and quotes only).
- **Poll / checklist**: tick boxes the reader scores themselves on; "vote in the comments".
- **Medal / badge hero**: the hexagon as a medal for award posts (use the real badge once it exists).
- **Kudos feed**: a chat or feed of shout-outs (invented first names only).

## Making it stop the scroll
- One real-world hero takes 40–60% of the canvas; everything else supports it.
- The headline names an agency moment or opens a gap, and makes sense on first read.
- Main read under ~12 words; detail goes in the caption.
- Shrink the PNG to ~300 px wide: the headline still reads and the idea is clear.
- Depth, not flatness: overlap, crop off the edge, soft shadows, the red glow behind the hero.
