# Vissey's taste guide

Rules learned from Vissey's feedback on real AWA posts. Read before making any post; the review checks against it too. Source: `playbook/taste.md` in the awa-posts project (keep the two in sync).

How to add a rule:
- One short rule per line, written as an instruction ("Max 2 lines of body text on statics").
- Put it under the right heading, with the post it came from in parentheses: `(from 2026-11-10_tof_perks-report-card)`.
- Love counts too: "love this hook" becomes a "do more of this" rule.
- If new feedback contradicts an old rule, update the old rule instead of adding a second one, and keep both sources.
- One-off content fixes (a typo, a wrong date) aren't taste. Don't add those.

## Copy and hooks
- Hooks must make sense on first read: name who and what ("your team", "the pitch"), no vague "it"/"they". Never squeeze a hook so tight it stops being clear. (from 2026-11-10_tof_only-when-wrong)
- Make every post clearly about agency life and recognition: use real agency moments (pitches, launches, clients, briefs) and tie back to people being seen. (from 2026-11-10_tof_only-when-wrong)
- Lean positive: show what good looks like, not only what's broken. A problem post should land on the better way, but never bend a device's logic to force it (outlined ghost words = "never said", and the labels must agree). (from 2026-11-10_tof_only-when-wrong)
- CTAs spell out the action: "Click the link in our bio to see how agencies get rated", not a bare "See how...". (from CP-012)

## Layout and density
- Type on flat color alone feels flat. Every post needs a real-world element (photo, device, paper, UI) or strong depth (overlap, shadows, cropping off the edge). (from 2026-11-10_tof_only-when-wrong)
- Prefer real photos of people over flat illustrations: a cut-out team photo overlapping the panel edge beats a faceless figure. Keep the cut-out clear of text and pills. (from CP-004, Vissey's Canva edit)
- Keep headline lines, subline and CTA as one tight block (headline lines nearly touching). Give the logo a smaller size and its own breathing room above. (from CP-002, Vissey's Canva edit)
- CTA slides stay lean: statement + one CTA + the URL. No extra subline between them. (from CP-012)
- Max 3 text levels per static. Extra lines like a "send this" CTA go in the caption, not on the design. (from 2026-11-10_tof_only-when-wrong)

## Type and color
- Default to a soft look for all graphics, not flat and hard: a dark background with a red glow rising from below, blur for depth, soft shadows and halos, and rounded Poppins headlines (bold + light mix) over condensed Anton. (from CP-002, chosen over CP-002-original)
- Outlined/ghost text must still read on a phone: thick, clearly visible stroke, never a faint hairline. (from 2026-11-10_tof_only-when-wrong)
- No Caveat script inside headlines. For an accent word in a headline, use Poppins (light italic in red works). Keep Caveat for handwritten notes and stickers only. (from CP-002)
- Alternate the feed: if the previous post was dark dominant, make the next post's cover (slide 1) white dominant, and vice versa. Later slides can mix. (from CP-012)

## Logo and brand
- On dark backgrounds, use the logo with white words (`awa-logo-light`). (from the logo setup, 2026-11-09)

## Formats and references
- When there's no real photo, a UI device (phone mid-scroll, careers page) beats a flat illustrated person. Saved as poster-scroll-phone. (from CP-003)
