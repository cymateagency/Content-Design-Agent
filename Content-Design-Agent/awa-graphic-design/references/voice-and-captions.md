# Voice and captions

## AWA voice
- Talks to agency owners, founders and leaders.
- Direct, warm, a little cheeky. Short sentences. Truth over hype.
- Says "your team", "your people", "your agency".
- Lean positive: name the problem, then land on what good looks like.
- Avoid: "synergy", "leverage", "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", and emoji in designs.
- Recognition, retention, trust, growth and being seen are the core themes.
- **No em dashes** anywhere (captions or on-image text). Use a comma or full stop instead. QA flags any it finds.

## Hard rules (facts and people)
- Never invent statistics, studies or percentages. Numbers come only from data the user provides or a source they name; otherwise use a non-numeric line or leave `XX` placeholders and flag them.
- Never invent testimonials or quotes, and never put words in a real person's mouth. Real names, titles and quotes only when the user provides them.
- Invented first names are fine inside fictional formats (lock screen, letter, kudos feed), never with job titles or company names.
- One idea per static. One idea per carousel slide.

## Caption format
Write the Instagram caption in this exact shape (it doubles as the post's `caption.txt`, with the LinkedIn version added below it):
```
STAGE: TOF | FORMAT: poster-lockscreen   (or for a fresh layout: FORMAT: new / <short layout name>)

<hook line, under 12 words>

<2-4 short lines of body>

<CTA that matches the stage>

<3-5 hashtags, always include #AgencyWorkplaces>

LINKEDIN:
<hook line>

<4-8 short lines of body: same idea, a little more context and the "why">

<CTA that matches the stage, with "link in the comments">

<3-5 hashtags, always include #AgencyWorkplaces>

---
Photos needed: <list, or "none">
Canva:
<static: "slide 1: <link>"   |   carousel: "carousel (<n> pages): <link>">  (only if the post went to Canva)
```
- The hook's first line must work alone before "…more".
- The CTA spells out the action ("Click the link in our bio to see how agencies get rated") and matches the stage (`funnel.md`) and the CTA on the design.
- When delivering, show each caption (hook to hashtags) in its own code block so each copies in one click.

## Platform versions
Every post gets both versions.
- **Instagram**: short. Hook, 2–4 short body lines, CTA ("link in bio" when there's a link), hashtags.
- **LinkedIn**: longer. Same hook and idea, 4–8 short lines with a bit more context and the reasoning, line breaks between short paragraphs, the link goes in the comments ("link in the comments"), hashtags.
- Same CTA intent and same facts in both, matching the design.
