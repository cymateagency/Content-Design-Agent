# Cymate graphic design

Ken runs content for Cymate, a US-based B2B cold outbound agency. Cymate's socials (Instagram @cymate.io and LinkedIn) are there to win customers, not just followers, and the content focuses on GTM: cold email, deliverability, list building, reply rates and booked meetings. This skill turns a request like "carousel on list segmentation" or "static from this transcript" into finished graphics plus the caption that goes with them.

The brand should feel premium and high-production but never stiff: dynamic, fun, stylish or realistic depending on the idea. Every post has to make someone stop scrolling and read, so trend-led and pop-culture hooks are welcome. Earlier rounds were sent back for being too minimalist or robotic, so every design needs real substance and a production-ready finish. Only finished work is shown.

## Craft standard
Every asset should look designed by hand in Photoshop by a senior designer, never generated or robotic, and be perfectly accurate. Read `references/craft.md` before building anything: build in layers, one light source with consistent two-layer shadows, real materials and photos, designer-grade typography, a strict grid, none of the AI tells. Rendering adds a finishing pass and a QA pass automatically.

## Tagline
Cymate’s official tagline is **“Outbound for Champions”**, but it is optional: use it only where it genuinely adds something (channel art, a brand moment), not by default. Most assets should carry their own headline and idea. The official LinkedIn banner (`assets/inspiration/08-…`) defines the “Champions” look; see `references/inspiration.md` #8. Beyond that, use your own judgment on concepts and copy, as long as everything fits the brand standard below.

## Brand standard (applies to every asset)
Keep the company brand professional, sharp, clean, and a little fun. We want enterprise buyers to trust us on sight. That means polished visuals, clear writing, and proof we know our stuff.

- **Professional and sharp**: every asset should look like it came from a top agency's in-house team. Crisp edges, aligned grids, consistent spacing, nothing cramped, cluttered or amateur.
- **Clean**: one idea, one hero element, generous breathing room. Cut anything that doesn't earn its place.
- **A little fun**: a wink in the copy, a playful prop or the mascot in a cameo. Humour supports the point; it never cheapens the brand, punches down or turns into clickbait.
- **Trust on sight**: picture a VP of Sales at a large company seeing it in their feed. If it would make them doubt Cymate's credibility, rework it.
- **Proof we know our stuff**: teach something specific and correct (a real mechanism, a concrete tactic, the right terms). Use only real numbers and results, never invented ones (see `voice-and-captions.md`).

## Workflow

Follow these steps in order. The duplicate check (step 2) and the visual check (step 5) are never skipped.

### 1. Pin down the request
Work out from the message:
- **Format**: read `references/formats.md`. "A post" or "a graphic" with no format means the 1080 × 1350 static. "A Reel", "a video" or "a transition" means the animated Reel: also read `references/reels.md`.
- **Platform**: Instagram, LinkedIn or both. It changes the carousel output and the caption. If unsaid, make assets that work on both and give both caption versions.
- **Topic and source**: a topic, a draft caption, a transcript, a doc or a screenshot. Pull the core problem, the reframe and any proof points (numbers, results) from the source. If there's no topic, pick one using `references/trends.md` and the content log rather than stopping to ask.
- **Trend research**: about once every 3–4 posts (check the content log for the last trend-led one), and whenever asked, run a web search for what the whole world is talking about right now and build the hook from it (`references/trends.md`). Not every post; the rest can be evergreen.
- **Background**: decide whether a stock still or clip would help (about one post in three). If so, follow `references/backgrounds.md`: suggest 2–3 licensed picks with links, or use a file the user supplied, at 20–30% opacity.
- **CTA**: link in bio, link in comments or booking link. If it isn't clear, keep a bracketed placeholder and flag it rather than guessing.
- **Wesley**: if the post should feature him, use a photo the user supplied (`--photo`, ideally a transparent cutout PNG) or `assets/wesley.png` if bundled. If neither exists, ask for one rather than leaving him out silently.

### 2. Check for duplicates
Read `references/content-log.md`, then search past chats (conversation_search with terms like "Cymate carousel", "Cymate static", "Cymate thumbnail" or the topic) for anything made since the log was updated. A repeat of the same topic with the same angle and visual is a duplicate; a new angle on a known topic is fine (say what's different). Resizing an existing graphic into another format references the original ID instead of counting as a duplicate.

Tell the user in one line what you checked.

### 3. Plan the concept
Read `references/brand-and-design.md` and `references/styles.md` before designing. Decide:
- **The hook**: what makes someone stop. For trend-led posts or when no topic is given, follow `references/trends.md` (search what’s trending this week, find the bridge to an outbound truth, keep it IP-safe).
- **The style**: the one in `styles.md` that makes the idea land fastest, different from the most recent post in the content log. Fun, dynamic, realistic, stylish or premium are all on the table; match the mood to the message.
- **The hero element**: the one thing that takes 40–60% of the canvas (a number, a realistic UI, a prop, a chart, a person, huge type).
- **Concept rotation**: pick a device from `references/rotation-library.md` (zoom reveal, page peel, notebook on grid, calculator punchline, bait-and-switch, break-the-frame, etc.) that hasn't been used in the last 5 posts in the content log.
- **Look**: the approved direction is in `references/inspiration.md` (glass steps over orange light, spec-sheet callouts, object puns on off-white, big type with props, floating glass around a device, sketch over photo). Borrow the touch, keep the Cymate DNA (header, orange gradient, type, social bar).
- **Background**: near-black with a faint charcoal lift and warm ember glow by default (no blue anywhere), plus at most one texture (a stock background at 20–30% opacity counts as the texture). Burnt orange occasionally (about one post in four); off-white `cm paper` for object-pun and big-type posts (about one in five). Never solid blue.
- **The headline and its 1–3 gradient words.**

For a batch, give each post a different style. For carousels, write the slide outline first (cover hook, one point per middle slide, takeaway + CTA) and the motion for each slide. Every carousel is animated by default: one MP4 per slide so it plays as people swipe (`references/animated-carousels.md`). For Reels, write the beat sheet first (hook, 2–3 teaching beats, transition style, pivot, end card, with timings) per `reels.md`.

### 4. Build it
Copy the matching template from `templates/`, then replace the content and props. `{{BASE_CSS}}`, `{{HEADER}}` (wordmark + mascot icon) and `{{SOCIAL_BAR}}` are inlined by the renderer, so every post keeps the same frame; don’t hand-edit those partials for a single post. Build props in HTML/CSS/inline SVG with believable proportions, soft shadows and consistent light. Use `.grad-text`, `.chrome-text`, `.kpill`, `.gpill`, `.glass` and `var(--accent)` from the base CSS. Draw arrows and icons as SVG. Logo tokens: `{{LOGO}}`, `{{ICON}}` (small mascot), `{{MASCOT}}` (large mascot), `{{LOCKUP}}` (stacked logo), `{{MONOGRAM}}` (orange tile); the full official set is in `assets/brand/`. Never use cyan, the old hex icon, or a retyped wordmark.

Render:
```bash
# single images (add --width/--height for other formats; see formats.md)
python3 scripts/render.py path/to/post.html out/name.png
python3 scripts/render.py path/to/post.html out/name.png --photo /mnt/user-data/uploads/wesley.png   # person style
python3 scripts/render.py path/to/post.html out/name.png --bg /mnt/user-data/uploads/team.jpg        # photo background
python3 scripts/render.py path/to/post.html out/name.png --bg /mnt/user-data/uploads/stock.jpg --bg-opacity 0.25  # stock layer (.tx-stock)

# carousels: one HTML per slide (01.html, 02.html, ...) in a folder; extra flags pass through
python3 scripts/make_carousel.py path/to/slides/ out/carousel-name.pdf --video   # animated MP4 per slide + PNGs + PDF (default)

# reels: animated HTML -> MP4 + cover PNG (+ contact sheet with --preview)
python3 scripts/make_reel.py path/to/reel.html out/reel-name.mp4 --preview      # music bed + SFX from the template's meta cues
python3 scripts/make_reel.py path/to/reel.html out/reel-name.mp4 --bg-video /mnt/user-data/uploads/stock.mp4 --bg-opacity 0.25 --preview
```
The scripts install the fonts on first run (the emoji font only when a design uses emoji), embed the logo assets, and print WARN lines for text that spills out of the canvas or its box.

### 5. Look at it, then fix it
Every render is supersampled, finished like a Photoshop export and checked by `qa.py` (spelling, curly quotes, em dashes, widows, small text, edge margins, overlaps, near-miss alignment, contrast). Fix every WARN or confirm it is deliberate. Then judge the craft against `references/craft.md`: layered depth, one consistent light, two-layer shadows, real materials, no AI tells.
Open every PNG (every slide for carousels; for Reels the contact sheet plus frames at the hook, a teaching beat, the transition and the end card) with the view tool and check it against `references/quality-checklist.md`. Fix and re-render until it passes. Treat every WARN line as a problem until you've seen it isn't one.

### 6. Write the copy
Follow `references/voice-and-captions.md`: the four-part structure with no visible labels, no em dashes, short sentences, platform-specific length, link placement and hashtags. Reels get a caption, the music style used and an optional trending sound to pair with the SFX-only file (never bake in music we don't own). Thumbnails get 2–3 title options instead of a caption (YouTube descriptions belong to the `cymate-shortform-description` skill). Banners get alt text only. Never invent numbers or client results.

### 7. Deliver
Copy the finished files to `/mnt/user-data/outputs/` and present them (for carousels: the slide MP4s in order, then the PDF and PNGs; for Reels: the full-mix MP4 first, then the SFX-only MP4 and the cover PNG). For each graphic give:
- **File(s)**
- **Format and platform**
- **Caption(s)**, ready to paste (LinkedIn and/or Instagram)
- **Alt text**: one plain sentence
- **Note** (only if needed): placeholders to fill, a CTA to confirm, illustrative names, the trend used and why, the stock source and licence, or the suggested sound

Keep the message short; the files and copy are the work.

### 8. Keep the log current
After delivering, give the new lines for `references/content-log.md` in its format and mention in one sentence that adding them to the skill keeps the duplicate check accurate.

## Reference files
- `references/formats.md`: sizes, templates, render flags, safe zones and copy type per format. Read at step 1.
- `references/brand-and-design.md`: background, colours, logo, social bar, type, signature elements, Wesley rules, direction. Read before every design.
- `references/styles.md`: the style library and scroll-stopping rules. Read at step 3.
- `references/trends.md`: global-trend cadence, picking trends, evergreen formats, calendar hooks, IP-safe pop culture. Read for trend-led posts, when there’s no topic, or when the cadence says it’s time.
- `references/craft.md`: the hand-made, Photoshop-grade standard (layers, light and shadow, materials, typography, grid, AI tells to avoid, finish pass, accuracy checklist). Read for every asset.
- `references/rotation-library.md`: 16 reviewed reference posts (Instagram + Pinterest), the device to borrow from each and a Cymate angle; rotate so no device repeats within 5 posts. Read when choosing a concept.
- `references/inspiration.md`: the seven approved reference posts, the touch to borrow from each, and the matching templates. Read before planning any static post; view the reference image for the chosen direction.
- `references/animated-carousels.md`: animated carousels (default): motion utilities, timing, cover rule, sound, render and check. Read for any carousel.
- `references/gifs.md`: simple seamless-loop GIFs for LinkedIn (conveyor/station scenes), specs, loop rules, `make_gif.py`. Read for any GIF.
- `references/reels.md`: Reel structure (educate then pivot), transition library, music styles and SFX cues, safe zones, render and check. Read for any Reel or video.
- `references/backgrounds.md`: stock stills and footage at 20–30% opacity, licensed sources, Pinterest caveat, getting files in. Read when using a background.
- `references/voice-and-captions.md`: Cymate voice, facts rule, the four-part caption, platform versions, hashtags. Read before writing.
- `references/content-log.md`: everything made or posted so far, with style and background. Read before choosing a concept.
- `references/quality-checklist.md`: pass/fail checks for step 5.
