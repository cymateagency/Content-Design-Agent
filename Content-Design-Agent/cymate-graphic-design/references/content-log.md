# Content log

Every Cymate graphic made with this skill. Check new ideas against **Topic/idea** and **Visual** before designing, and use **Style** to keep variety, then also search past chats for anything newer.

Format: `ID | Format | Style | Background | Topic/idea | Visual | Headline | Status`
Style is one of the styles in `styles.md` (or a short name for a new one); don’t repeat the previous post’s style. Background is `black`, `orange` or `light` (older posts: `navy`).
IDs: `P##` static posts, `CR##` carousels, `T##` thumbnails, `S##` stories/reel covers, `B##` banners. Status: `made`, `posted`, `retired`.
