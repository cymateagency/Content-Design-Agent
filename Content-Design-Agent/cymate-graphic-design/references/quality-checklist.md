# Quality checklist

View every rendered PNG (every slide for carousels) and fix anything that fails before the user sees it. Early Cymate rounds were sent back for looking too minimalist or robotic; these checks catch that.

## Layout
- [ ] render.py printed no WARN lines (or each one was checked and is intentional).
- [ ] Frame matches the template: brackets, kicker position, footer and margins.
- [ ] Headline within its line limit, no orphan word or orphan punctuation on the last line.
- [ ] One clear focal point; no big dead zones, no crowding.
- [ ] Enough substance: a visual (chart, stat card, comparison, tags, framed example) supports the headline, unless the format is a pure-type cover or thumbnail.
- [ ] Safe zones respected for stories, thumbnails and banners (`formats.md`).

## Brand
- [ ] Background is near-black with a faint charcoal lift and warm ember (or burnt orange / paper). No blue anywhere, nothing that pulls the eye.
- [ ] Header present: official white wordmark top-left, mascot top-right, undistorted. No hex icon, no retyped wordmark, no homemade horizontal lockup. Social bar on single posters and the last carousel slide.
- [ ] Gradient (`#C21A00 → #FF5A00 → #FFC09A`) used on 1–3 key words or the hero number, not everything. **No cyan.**
- [ ] One display face plus Poppins/Inter; no browser default font showing.
- [ ] One texture only; photos darkened, stock backgrounds at 20–30% opacity and never pulling the eye before the headline.
- [ ] Style differs from the most recent post in the content log.

## Scroll-stop test
- [ ] One clear hero element; main read under ~12 words.
- [ ] Shrunk to ~300 px wide, the headline still reads and the idea is clear.
- [ ] The headline opens a gap or names a pain; it isn’t a generic statement.
- [ ] Trend posts: the reference is current and recognisable, uses no borrowed characters, stills, logos or celebrity photos, and the outbound bridge lands in one glance.

## Carousels
- [ ] Cover works on its own (hook, no body paragraph).
- [ ] Ghost numerals and progress dots correct on every slide.
- [ ] Same frame and background family on every slide.
- [ ] Last-slide CTA matches the caption's hard CTA.
- [ ] PDF opens with slides in order (LinkedIn version).

## Reels
- [ ] Carousel MP4s are 1080 × 1350 with square pixels, identical to the PNG slides.
- [ ] Contact sheet and frames at hook, teaching beat, transition and end card all viewed.
- [ ] Movement in the first 0.3 s; hook readable muted; each beat on screen ≥ 1.8 s.
- [ ] Education works on its own; pivot ties to the lesson; CTA matches the caption.
- [ ] Text inside the Reels safe zones.
- [ ] Sound: every visible motion has a matching cue, the drop lands on the transition (check the spectrogram), about −14 LUFS, peak under −1 dB; both full-mix and SFX-only files exported; no outside music unless licensed.

## Craft (see craft.md)
- [ ] render.py printed no WARN lines, or each one is confirmed deliberate.
- [ ] Looks hand-built: layered depth, one light direction, contact + ambient shadows, real materials.
- [ ] No AI tells: not everything centred, no glow blobs everywhere, no sparkle emoji, no vague copy, no banding.
- [ ] Every word proofread by eye; handles, facts, numbers and CTA match the caption.

## Brand standard
- [ ] Looks professional, sharp and clean: polished finish, aligned, uncluttered, one clear hero.
- [ ] A little fun, never cheap: humour supports the point, nothing offensive, nothing clickbait.
- [ ] An enterprise buyer would trust it on sight.
- [ ] Shows real expertise: something specific and correct is taught or proven; no invented numbers.

## Text
- [ ] Spelling checked; curly quotes and apostrophes ( ’ “ ” ); no em dashes; no double spaces.
- [ ] Every number comes from the brief or source content; placeholders flagged.

## Copy
- [ ] Four-part caption with no visible labels; first line works as a hook on its own.
- [ ] Platform version(s) correct (length, hashtags, link placement).
- [ ] Not a duplicate of something in the content log.
