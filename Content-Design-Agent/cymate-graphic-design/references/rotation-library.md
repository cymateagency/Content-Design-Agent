# Rotation library (reference posts reviewed Nov 2026)

Sixteen posts the team picked (8 Instagram, 8 Pinterest), reviewed in the browser. Each entry names the **device** worth borrowing and a **Cymate angle** for it. Rotate through them: before planning a post, check the content log for the last 5 devices used and pick one that isn't among them. Borrow the device, never the layout, copy, product or brand. Everything still follows the brand standard (no blue, Cymate fonts, official logo, professional with a little fun).

## Patterns across all 16
- **Reveal mechanics win**: zoom/magnify (R2, P3), peel (P8), break-the-frame (R5), bait-and-switch (R6, R8). Plan at least one reveal per batch.
- **One colour, fully committed** (R4, P2, P3, P8): when a post goes orange, everything goes orange.
- **Real objects + simple drawing** (R3, P1, P6): photographed or realistic props with hand-drawn line art read as human-made.
- **Micro labels and handles** (R1, P2): tiny tracked labels, page counters, selection handles signal polish.
- **Comment-keyword CTAs** (R5, R7): “Comment X to get Y” drives engagement; only promise things the team can actually send.
